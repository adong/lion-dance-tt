THE LION DANCE
🥁 🦁 🌸 🥬 🧧

At Chinese New Year, two dancers become one lion: one is the head, one is the tail.

Drums, cymbals and gongs wake the lion up, and it dances to bring everyone good luck.

It leaps across the plum-blossom poles, then plucks the green: a lettuce and a red envelope, for a lucky new year!
